04 — stopping corruption

make cheating a losing game.

A country cannot be run on the hope that everyone stays honest. It can be built so that corruption is irrational — so that the expected cost of cheating exceeds the gain, even when ten crore is on the table.

scroll
The army does not just find strong people — it makes them. Ordinary recruits walk in; strong soldiers walk out.

Honest institutions are built the same way. Auditors, police and judges are selected for integrity and competence at the gate, and then training, culture and protected careers make integrity the ordinary way of working rather than an act of heroism.

Around those people, the structure is arranged so that a fraud succeeds only if several independent safeguards fail simultaneously.

the enforcement architecture

six independent safeguards.
a scam has to defeat all of them
at the same time.

mechanism 01

randomised assignment

Which auditor examines which office is decided by lottery, at the last possible moment, with frequent rotation. An official cannot buy an inspector whose identity nobody knows in advance.

mechanism 02

blind redundancy

A fraction of accounts are examined twice, by two teams unaware of each other's existence. A divergence between the two reports means either an error or a purchase. Corruption does not merely fail here — it announces itself.

mechanism 03

adversarial incentives

Auditors are paid more for substantiated findings, and whoever exposes a fraud receives a share of what is recovered. The arithmetic is deliberate: defecting from a conspiracy has to pay better than joining one.

mechanism 04

permanent public attribution

Who audited what, and who cleared whom, stays on the public record permanently. If an office signed off as clean later collapses in scandal, that signature remains attached to the name that made it. Anomalies are flagged automatically — the auditor who never finds anything is itself a finding.

mechanism 05

symmetric skin in the game

Severe consequences for auditors who join a fraud, and equally firm protection for those who expose one and make powerful enemies: fixed terms, and transfers only by a panel obliged to publish its reasoning. Where exposing a minister ends a career, the state ends up with auditors who find nothing.

mechanism 06

protection against intimidation

Genuine security for judges, auditors and witnesses under threat, arranged before an attempt rather than after one. The first intimidation to succeed teaches every subsequent criminal that intimidation works. The first to fail teaches the opposite, just as durably.

None of this needs saints. It only needs ordinary people responding to rewards and consequences — the one thing about human beings you can always count on.

and at the very top

who watches the watchers?

At the apex sits one small body overseeing the overseers — maximally visible, randomly rotated, changed often. No system eliminates the final residue of trust, and this one does not claim to. What it does is shrink the residue: from trusting thousands of officers individually, to trusting that a single small floodlit body is not captured entirely at once. That is the honest limit, and stating it is part of the design.
